exp-check 评测案例（人工答案版）

文件说明：
- 案例1到20各埋了若干问题，"埋的问题"是人工答案，按意思对应rules.md里的规则编号。
- 案例21到25是干净案例，期望输出为"无重大问题"，专门用来统计误报。
- "注意"一栏列的是看起来像问题、但不应被报的点，用于误报统计。
- 输入一律是英文的方法段落或图注，模拟真实稿件。

案例1：siRNA只用一条序列、没对照、只看mRNA
输入：
HeLa cells were transfected with 50 nM siRNA targeting GENE-X using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX. After 48 h, knockdown was confirmed by qPCR (70% reduction in GENE-X mRNA, n = 2). GENE-X-depleted cells showed a 45% reduction in proliferation measured by CellTiter-Glo at 72 h, indicating that GENE-X is required for HeLa cell growth.
埋的问题：
只用了一条siRNA，不能排除脱靶；没有non-targeting对照siRNA（通篇没提对照组是什么）；只验证了mRNA，没验证蛋白；n=2且没有统计检验；没有rescue，"required for growth"结论过强
---

案例2：shRNA稳定株用未转导细胞当对照
输入：
To generate stable knockdown lines, MCF7 cells were transduced with lentiviral pLKO.1-shGENE-Y and selected with puromycin (2 µg/ml) for 7 days. Untransduced MCF7 cells served as control. Knockdown efficiency was 80% by qPCR. shGENE-Y cells migrated 3-fold slower in transwell assays (n = 3 wells, p < 0.01, t-test).
埋的问题：
对照应为非靶向shRNA（如pLKO.1-scramble）并经同样的转导和puromycin筛选，未转导细胞控制不了慢病毒转导和筛选本身的影响；只有一条shRNA；n=3 wells是同一次实验的技术重复，不是独立生物学重复；蛋白水平未验证
---

案例3：线虫RNAi喂食，克隆未验证、打分未盲
输入：
Synchronized L1 worms were fed HT115 bacteria expressing dsRNA against gene-z from the Ahringer library. Worms fed bacteria carrying the empty L4440 vector served as control. At day 1 of adulthood, brood size was scored and gene-z(RNAi) animals showed a 35% reduction in brood size. The identity of the RNAi clone was assumed from its position in the library plate.
埋的问题：
RNAi克隆未经测序确认（Ahringer库存在错标克隆）；没有验证敲低（qPCR或已知阳性对照）；没有报告动物数量和独立重复次数；打分未盲法；没有统计
注意：L4440空载体对照是正确的，不应报
---

案例4：CRISPR敲除单guide单克隆
输入：
GENE-A knockout HEK293T cells were generated by transfecting px459 encoding a single sgRNA targeting exon 2. After puromycin selection, single clones were isolated, and one clone (KO-7) with a frameshift deletion detected by PCR genotyping was used for all subsequent experiments. Parental HEK293T cells served as the wild-type control. KO-7 cells displayed a 2-fold increase in apoptosis under serum starvation.
埋的问题：
只用一条sgRNA；只用一个克隆，无法区分基因效应和克隆差异；PCR基因分型不能确认移码，应测序两个等位基因并在蛋白水平确认缺失；没有脱靶评估；对照应包括非靶向sgRNA经同样筛选和单克隆化的对照克隆，而不只是亲本细胞；没有通过重新表达GENE-A做rescue；没有n和统计
---

案例5：CRISPRi用亲本细胞当对照
输入：
For CRISPRi, A549 cells stably expressing dCas9-KRAB were transduced with an sgRNA targeting the GENE-B transcription start site. GENE-B expression was reduced by 85% (qPCR). Compared with parental A549 cells, GENE-B-repressed cells showed increased sensitivity to cisplatin (IC50 2.1 µM vs 6.4 µM).
埋的问题：
对照应为dCas9-KRAB加非靶向sgRNA的细胞，而不是亲本A549，dCas9-KRAB表达和慢病毒转导本身可能改变药敏；只用一条sgRNA；IC50没有重复次数和置信区间；蛋白水平未验证
---

案例6：rescue构建不抗siRNA
输入：
To confirm specificity, GENE-X knockdown cells (siRNA #1) were transfected with a plasmid encoding full-length human GENE-X cDNA (pcDNA3.1-GENE-X) or empty vector. Re-expression of GENE-X partially restored proliferation (from 45% to 70% of control).
埋的问题：
rescue构建没有做成siRNA抗性（靶区同义突变），siRNA会同时敲低外源cDNA，所谓部分rescue可能只是过表达量压过了敲低；没有检测rescue条件下GENE-X实际蛋白水平；仍只针对一条siRNA；没有n和统计
---

案例7：缺氧实验用GAPDH做内参，技术复孔当n
输入：
Cells were cultured under hypoxia (1% O2) or normoxia for 24 h. Target gene expression was quantified by qPCR using the ΔΔCt method with GAPDH as the reference gene. Each condition was run in technical triplicate (n = 3), and VEGFA was induced 4.2-fold under hypoxia (p < 0.001).
埋的问题：
GAPDH本身受缺氧诱导，不适合作缺氧实验的内参，应验证内参稳定性或用多个内参；技术三复孔被当成n=3，缺独立生物学重复；统计建立在伪重复上；引物效率和熔解曲线未报告
---

案例8：Western没有上样对照
输入：
Protein lysates were separated by SDS-PAGE and probed with anti-GENE-C antibody. GENE-C protein was reduced by approximately 3-fold in knockdown cells relative to control, as shown in the representative blot (Figure 2B).
埋的问题：
没有上样对照或总蛋白染色；只有代表性图，没有多次独立重复的定量和统计；3倍的定量方法未说明（是否在线性范围内）；抗体来源和验证未提
---

案例9：动物实验没有vehicle、没随机、没盲法
输入：
Male C57BL/6 mice (8 weeks) received compound Z (10 mg/kg, i.p., daily) or no treatment for 14 days (n = 4 untreated, n = 6 treated). Tumor volume was measured by calipers by the investigator administering the treatment. Compound Z reduced tumor volume by 40% (p = 0.03, unpaired t-test).
埋的问题：
没有vehicle对照，控制不了溶剂和注射操作本身；没有随机分组；测量者就是给药者，未盲；两组样本量不等且没有样本量依据；只用雄性；只报终点，没有基线和时间过程；伦理批准未提
---

案例10：单一抑制剂、单一浓度、没溶剂对照
输入：
To test whether kinase K mediates the response, cells were treated with the kinase K inhibitor KI-1 (10 µM) for 24 h. KI-1 abolished the phosphorylation of substrate S and blocked cell migration, demonstrating that kinase K is required for migration.
埋的问题：
没有溶剂（DMSO）对照；单一浓度且10 µM偏高，没有剂量效应；没有结构不同的第二种抑制剂或遗传手段（siRNA、敲除）佐证，无法排除抑制剂脱靶；没有评估细胞毒性对迁移结果的影响；没有n和统计；"demonstrating"结论过强
---

案例11：CUT&Tag声称全局下降却用深度归一化
输入：
CUT&Tag for H3K27me3 was performed in control and GENE-D knockdown cells (one replicate each). Reads were normalized to sequencing depth (CPM). H3K27me3 signal was globally reduced across the genome in knockdown cells, indicating that GENE-D is required for Polycomb-mediated silencing.
埋的问题：
每组只有一个重复，无法评估重现性；没有IgG阴性对照；声称全基因组整体下降却用CPM深度归一化，这种归一化会抹平全局变化，需要spike-in归一化；抗体特异性未验证；结论过度延伸到Polycomb机制
---

案例12：RNA-seq用未校正p值，用同一批样本"验证"
输入：
RNA-seq was performed on control and treated cells (n = 2 per group). Genes with p < 0.05 (DESeq2 Wald test) were considered differentially expressed, yielding 1,842 DEGs. Three DEGs were validated by qPCR in the same RNA samples used for sequencing.
埋的问题：
用未校正的p值而非FDR校正后的padj，1842个DEG里大量假阳性；n=2偏少；qPCR验证用的是测序的同一批RNA，不是独立样本，只能算技术确认；批次设计未说明
---

案例13：荧光素酶报告基因没有转染效率归一化
输入：
HEK293 cells were transfected with the GENE-E promoter firefly luciferase reporter together with siRNA against TF-1 or control siRNA. After 48 h, firefly luciferase activity was measured and normalized to total protein. TF-1 knockdown reduced promoter activity by 60% (n = 3 wells).
埋的问题：
没有内参报告基因（如Renilla）归一化转染效率，总蛋白控制不了转染效率差异；缺少TF-1结合位点突变的启动子或空载体对照来说明特异性；n=3 wells是技术重复；TF-1敲低效率未报告
---

案例14：成像定量伪重复、未盲
输入：
Cells were immunostained for marker M and imaged by confocal microscopy. Fluorescence intensity was quantified in ImageJ using a manually set threshold. Thirty cells from one coverslip per condition were analyzed. Marker M intensity was significantly higher in treated cells (p < 0.0001, t-test).
埋的问题：
30个细胞来自同一张盖玻片，是伪重复，n应为独立实验次数；人工设阈值且未盲，有主观偏倚；没有说明各条件成像参数一致；只做了一次实验；极小的p值正是伪重复的典型表现
---

案例15：Co-IP没有阴性对照和input
输入：
FLAG-tagged protein P and HA-tagged protein Q were co-overexpressed in HEK293T cells. Anti-FLAG immunoprecipitates were probed with anti-HA, revealing a band at the expected size, demonstrating that P and Q interact.
埋的问题：
没有阴性对照（单独表达HA-Q做FLAG-IP，或FLAG空载），不能排除HA-Q非特异结合beads或抗体；没有input泳道；只是过表达条件下的互作，没有内源水平验证；共IP不能区分直接和间接互作，"demonstrating"过强
---

案例16：过表达用未转染细胞当对照
输入：
To examine the effect of GENE-F, cells were transiently transfected with pCMV-GENE-F. Untransfected cells served as control. GENE-F overexpression increased cell migration 2.5-fold at 48 h.
埋的问题：
对照应为空载体转染，未转染细胞控制不了转染试剂和质粒本身的影响；没有验证GENE-F表达（Western）；转染效率未报告；没有n和统计
---

案例17：多组比较反复用t检验，p=0.06说成趋势
输入：
Cells were treated with vehicle, drug A, or drug B. Viability was compared between each drug and vehicle using unpaired t-tests (drug A vs vehicle p = 0.03; drug B vs vehicle p = 0.06, showing a trend toward significance). Data are mean ± SEM of n = 3.
埋的问题：
三组比较应用单因素方差分析加事后检验或做多重比较校正，而不是多次t检验；把p=0.06说成"趋势"是误读；n=3时应报SD并展示单个数据点；没说明n=3是独立实验还是复孔
---

案例18：试剂身份不明
输入：
Cells (obtained from a collaborator) were transfected with an siRNA against GENE-G and lysed 48 h later. Blots were probed with an anti-GENE-G antibody. GENE-G was efficiently depleted.
埋的问题：
siRNA序列或货号未给出；抗体来源、货号、RRID和验证情况未给出；细胞系没有STR鉴定和支原体检测；"efficiently depleted"没有定量；没有对照siRNA
---

案例19：对照取样时间不同
输入：
Cells were treated with cytokine C for 24 h. Expression of target T was compared between cytokine-treated cells harvested at 24 h and untreated cells harvested at 0 h. Cytokine C induced T expression 3-fold.
埋的问题：
对照在0h取样、处理组在24h取样，培养时间、密度、培养基消耗本身就会改变T的表达，应设24h未处理或vehicle对照；没有n和统计；没有剂量或时间过程
---

案例20：混合池当独立序列，表型时间点敲低已消退
输入：
GENE-H was silenced with a pool of four siRNAs (SMARTpool, 25 nM). Knockdown was 55% at the mRNA level at 48 h. Phenotypes were assessed 7 days after transfection. The pool, which contains four independent sequences, rules out off-target effects.
埋的问题：
混合池不等于分别验证的独立siRNA，不能排除脱靶，应至少两条单独siRNA各自重现表型；55%的敲低偏弱却被当成功能丧失；瞬时转染7天后敲低很可能已消退，应在表型时间点重新验证；蛋白水平未验证；没提non-targeting pool对照
---

案例21：干净案例，规范的siRNA实验
输入：
HeLa cells were transfected with two independent siRNAs targeting GENE-X (si-1 and si-2, 20 nM each; sequences in Table S1) or a non-targeting control siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01). Knockdown was confirmed at 48 h by Western blot (anti-GENE-X, Abcam ab12345, RRID:AB_123456; α-tubulin as loading control): 85% (si-1) and 78% (si-2) reduction. Both siRNAs reduced proliferation (CellTiter-Glo, 72 h) by 40–48%. Expression of siRNA-resistant GENE-X cDNA carrying five silent mutations in the si-1 target site, but not empty vector, restored proliferation in si-1-treated cells. Data are mean ± SD of three independent experiments; one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post hoc test versus control siRNA.
埋的问题：
无
注意：表型在72h、敲低验证在48h是常规做法，有两条siRNA和rescue佐证，不应报；序列放在补充表里不算缺失
---

案例22：干净案例，规范的CRISPR敲除
输入：
GENE-A knockout clones were generated in HEK293T cells using two independent sgRNAs targeting exons 2 and 3 (sequences in Table S1). Two clones per sgRNA were isolated; frameshift alleles were confirmed by Sanger sequencing of both alleles, and loss of protein was confirmed by Western blot. Control clones were derived in parallel using a non-targeting sgRNA and underwent the same selection and single-cell cloning. All four knockout clones showed a 1.8–2.3-fold increase in apoptosis under serum starvation, which was reversed by stable re-expression of sgRNA-resistant GENE-A. The top five predicted off-target sites for each sgRNA were sequenced and found unmodified. n = 3 independent experiments per clone; two-way ANOVA.
埋的问题：
无

案例23：干净案例，规范的动物实验
输入：
Eight-week-old C57BL/6J mice of both sexes were randomly assigned (block randomization by litter and sex) to vehicle (5% DMSO in saline, i.p.) or compound Z (10 mg/kg, i.p., daily) for 14 days, n = 10 per group. A sample size of 8 per group was calculated to detect a 30% difference with 80% power at α = 0.05 based on pilot variance; 10 were used to allow for attrition. Tumor volume was measured twice weekly by an investigator blinded to group allocation. The pre-specified primary endpoint was tumor volume at day 14, analyzed by unpaired t-test after confirming normality (Shapiro–Wilk); growth curves were analyzed by a mixed-effects model. All procedures were approved by the institutional IACUC (protocol #2026-0417).
埋的问题：
无

案例24：干净案例，规范的qPCR
输入：
Total RNA was extracted from three independent cultures per condition (biological replicates), DNase-treated, and reverse transcribed with random hexamers. qPCR was run in technical triplicate, which were averaged before analysis. Reference genes (RPLP0 and TBP) were selected from six candidates by geNorm as stable across conditions. Primer efficiencies were 95–104% and single products were confirmed by melt-curve analysis. Relative expression was calculated by the efficiency-corrected ΔΔCt method, and statistics were performed on ΔCt values (unpaired t-test, n = 3).
埋的问题：
无

案例25：干净案例，规范的CUT&Tag
输入：
CUT&Tag for H3K27me3 was performed in two biological replicates per condition with an IgG control, using an antibody validated by loss of signal in EZH2-inhibitor-treated cells. E. coli carry-over DNA was used for spike-in normalization to allow comparison of global H3K27me3 levels between conditions. Peaks were called with SEACR against the IgG control and retained only if reproducible between replicates (IDR < 0.05). Differential domains were identified with DESeq2 using spike-in-derived size factors (FDR < 0.05).
埋的问题：
无
