## Text

PANC-1 cells (ATCC CRL-1469; STR-profiled, mycoplasma-negative, passages 5-15) were transfected with 20 nM non-targeting siRNA (Horizon D-001810-01) or with siGENE-F-1 (J-004567-06) or siGENE-F-2 (J-004567-08) using DharmaFECT 1, identical volumes in all arms. After 24 h, half of the wells of each siRNA condition received compound Z (2 uM, dissolved in DMSO, 0.1% final) for a further 24 h, while the remaining wells were kept in plain growth medium without any addition. Knockdown measured in the assay lysates was 85 +/- 5% and 77 +/- 6% at the protein level (anti-GENE-F, Bethyl A300-123A, 1:2000; vinculin, Abcam ab129002, loading control). Viability (CellTiter-Blue) was measured in four independent transfections on separate days (n = 4 biological replicates); mean +/- SD. Data were analysed by two-way ANOVA (siRNA x compound) with the interaction reported (F(2,18) = 14.6, p = 0.0004) and Sidak-corrected post-hoc comparisons. An siRNA-resistant GENE-F cDNA abolished the sensitization.
