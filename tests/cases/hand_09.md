## Text

Male C57BL/6 mice (8 weeks) received compound Z (10 mg/kg, i.p., daily) or no treatment for 14 days (n = 4 untreated, n = 6 treated). Tumor volume was measured by calipers by the investigator administering the treatment. Compound Z reduced tumor volume by 40% (p = 0.03, unpaired t-test).
