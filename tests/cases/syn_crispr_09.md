## Text

Figure 5. CRISPRi silencing of GENE-J impairs oxidative phosphorylation in K562 cells. K562 cells stably expressing dCas9-KRAB (STR-authenticated; mycoplasma-negative; passages 8-19) were transduced with a non-targeting sgRNA (sgNT) or with sgJ-1 or sgJ-2, which bind distinct positions at the GENE-J TSS (hCRISPRi-v2 library), and selected identically with puromycin for 6 days. (A) RT-qPCR at day 8 (delta-delta-Ct; reference genes GAPDH and HPRT1, stability confirmed by NormFinder): 88 +/- 4% and 85 +/- 5% knockdown. (B) Western blot at the same time point showing loss of protein J (anti-GENE-J, Abcam ab67890, RRID:AB_2345678, 1:1000; vinculin loading control). (C) Basal oxygen consumption rate (Seahorse XFe96). (D) A CRISPRi-resistant GENE-J cDNA driven from a heterologous promoter, but not empty vector, restores oxygen consumption. Data are mean +/- SD of n = 4 independent transductions (biological replicates); one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's correction versus sgNT: p = 0.0011 (sgJ-1), p = 0.0025 (sgJ-2), p = 0.41 (rescue).
