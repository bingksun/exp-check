## Text

Figure 5. GENE-H is required for xenograft growth. Luciferase-labelled MDA-MB-231 cells (STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative) stably expressing doxycycline-inducible shGENE-H-1 (TRCN0000987654), shGENE-H-2 (TRCN0000987660; non-overlapping target sites) or non-targeting shRNA (Sigma SHC002), all produced, puromycin-selected and sorted in parallel, were injected into the fourth mammary fat pad of 8-week-old female NSG mice (n = 8 mice per group, block-randomised to cages and groups; IACUC protocol 2021-045). Caliper measurements and histological scoring were performed by an investigator blinded to group identity. Tumour volume over time was analysed by a linear mixed-effects model (group x time, mouse as random effect) with Holm-corrected contrasts: shGENE-H-1 p = 0.0007, shGENE-H-2 p = 0.0021 versus non-targeting at day 35. Points show mean +/- SEM. Endpoint tumour lysates showed 81% and 74% loss of GENE-H protein (Abcam ab98765, RRID:AB_345678, 1:1000, signal absent in knockout control; beta-actin loading control). An shRNA-1-resistant GENE-H cDNA restored growth (Fig. S5).
