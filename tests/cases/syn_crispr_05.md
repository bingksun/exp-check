## Text

MCF7 cells (ATCC HTB-22; STR-authenticated; mycoplasma-negative; passages 10-22) stably expressing the dCas9-VP64/MS2-p65-HSF1 SAM system were transduced with sgRNAs targeting the GENE-E promoter (sgE-1 and sgE-2, binding -112 and -248 relative to the TSS; designed in CRISPOR) or with a non-targeting sgRNA (sgNT), and selected with blasticidin (5 ug/ml, 6 days) in parallel. Induction was verified in the same cells and at the same time point by RT-qPCR (delta-delta-Ct; reference genes RPLP0 and TBP, stability confirmed by geNorm; 42- and 38-fold induction) and by western blot (anti-GENE-E, Abcam ab54321, RRID:AB_874512, 1:1000; beta-actin loading control). All three lines were then treated with 1 uM compound Z (Selleck S1234, dissolved in DMSO) for 24 h and viability was measured by CellTiter-Glo. Data are mean +/- SD of n = 4 independent transductions; the three groups were compared by pairwise unpaired two-tailed t-tests (sgE-1 vs sgNT p = 0.008; sgE-2 vs sgNT p = 0.011; sgE-1 vs sgE-2 p = 0.62); p values were not adjusted for multiple comparisons.
