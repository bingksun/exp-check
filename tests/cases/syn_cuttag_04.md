## Text

GENE-A knockdown and CUT&Tag. U2OS cells (ATCC HTB-96; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 4-12) were reverse-transfected with 20 nM of either of two non-overlapping siRNAs against GENE-A (Dharmacon J-012345-05, J-012345-07) or non-targeting siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01) using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX (Thermo Fisher 13778150) and processed 72 h later. Knockdown was checked by RT-qPCR 24 h after transfection (ddCt method; GAPDH and TBP as reference genes, stable across conditions). For rescue, cells stably expressing siRNA-resistant GENE-A cDNA (silent mutations in both target sites) or empty vector were transfected in parallel. Rescue protein matched endogenous GENE-A levels on western blots (Proteintech 12345-1-AP, RRID:AB_2123456, 1:1000; alpha-tubulin loading control, Sigma T9026). CUT&Tag used anti-H3K9me3 (Abcam ab8898, RRID:AB_306848; 1:100; validated on a SNAP-CUTANA K-MetStat panel), rabbit IgG (EpiCypher 13-0042; negative control) and anti-H3K4me3 (EpiCypher 13-0041; positive control). Three independent transfections per condition were sequenced and normalized to E. coli DNA. Peaks were called with SEACR and differential regions identified with DESeq2 (BH-adjusted P < 0.05) through one automated pipeline.
