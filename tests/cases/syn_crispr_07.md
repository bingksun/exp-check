## Text

Female NSG mice (NOD.Cg-Prkdcscid Il2rgtm1Wjl/SzJ, JAX 005557, 8-10 weeks, IACUC protocol 2021-045) received subcutaneous injections of 2x10^6 PANC-1-Cas9 cells carrying a non-targeting sgRNA (sgNT), sgG-1 or sgG-2 (exons 3 and 8; CRISPOR-designed, top five off-targets amplicon-sequenced), or sgG-1 plus an sgRNA-resistant GENE-G cDNA. Knockout was confirmed in the injected pools on the day of implantation by ICE analysis (>90% indels) and by western blot showing undetectable protein G (anti-GENE-G, Abcam ab98765, RRID:AB_10711223, 1:1000; HSP90 loading control). Mice were randomised to groups by a computer-generated list, and tumour volume was measured every 3 days for 21 days with calipers by an investigator blinded to group identity. Each group contained two mice. Tumour volumes at each time point were compared between sgNT and each other group by unpaired two-tailed t-tests; p values were not adjusted. Data are mean +/- SEM; p = 0.03 for sgG-1 at day 21.
