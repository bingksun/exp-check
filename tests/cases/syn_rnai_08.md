## Text

HepG2 cells (ATCC HB-8065; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 7-17) were transfected with 25 nM non-targeting siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01), siGENE-G-1 (J-005566-07) or siGENE-G-2 (J-005566-09) using RNAiMAX, all at the same concentration, volume and 72 h duration; siPLK1 (D-003290-01) was included as a positive control for transfection efficiency and assay response. Immunoblotting of the assay lysates showed 85% and 79% loss of protein P (anti-GENE-G, Abcam ab54321, RRID:AB_456789, 1:1000, signal absent in GENE-G knockout lysates; GAPDH, Proteintech 60004-1-Ig, loading control; non-saturated LI-COR scans). An siRNA-resistant GENE-G cDNA (silent mutations in the siGENE-G-1 site), expressed at 1.2-fold endogenous level with empty vector alongside, restored triglyceride accumulation. Oil Red O signal was quantified in five independent transfections on separate days from separate thaws (n = 5 biological replicates); bars show mean +/- SD. After confirming normality (Shapiro-Wilk) and equal variances, data were analysed by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's correction versus the non-targeting control (siGENE-G-1, p = 0.0012; siGENE-G-2, p = 0.0038).
