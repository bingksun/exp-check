## Text

Synchronized L1 worms were fed HT115 bacteria expressing dsRNA against gene-z from the Ahringer library. Worms fed bacteria carrying the empty L4440 vector served as control. At day 1 of adulthood, brood size was scored and gene-z(RNAi) animals showed a 35% reduction in brood size. The identity of the RNAi clone was assumed from its position in the library plate.

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): RNAi克隆未经测序确认（Ahringer库存在错标克隆）；没有验证敲低（qPCR或已知阳性对照）；没有报告动物数量和独立重复次数；打分未盲法；没有统计
- Must not be flagged (handwritten): L4440空载体对照是正确的，不应报
- 映射：RNAi克隆未测序确认→R12（试剂身份）；敲低未验证→R08；动物数和独立重复未报→R02；打分未盲→R05；没有统计→R03。不应报：L4440空载体对照是正确的（R01/R07不应被触发）。
