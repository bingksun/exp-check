## Text

Cells were treated with cytokine C for 24 h. Expression of target T was compared between cytokine-treated cells harvested at 24 h and untreated cells harvested at 0 h. Cytokine C induced T expression 3-fold.

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): 对照在0h取样、处理组在24h取样，培养时间、密度、培养基消耗本身就会改变T的表达，应设24h未处理或vehicle对照；没有n和统计；没有剂量或时间过程
- 映射：对照0h取样、处理组24h取样，应设时间匹配的对照→R01；没有n→R02；没有统计→R03。未映射：没有剂量或时间过程。
