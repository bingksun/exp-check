## Text

HEK293 cells were transfected with the GENE-E promoter firefly luciferase reporter together with siRNA against TF-1 or control siRNA. After 48 h, firefly luciferase activity was measured and normalized to total protein. TF-1 knockdown reduced promoter activity by 60% (n = 3 wells).

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): 没有内参报告基因（如Renilla）归一化转染效率，总蛋白控制不了转染效率差异；缺少TF-1结合位点突变的启动子或空载体对照来说明特异性；n=3 wells是技术重复；TF-1敲低效率未报告
- 映射：缺启动子突变体/空载体对照→R01；没有Renilla归一化转染效率→R12（归一化）；n=3 wells是技术重复→R02；TF-1敲低效率未报→R08。不应报：有control siRNA（R07不应被触发）。
