## Text

U2OS cells (ATCC HTB-96; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 12-20) were reverse-transfected for 72 h with 20 nM of either of two non-overlapping GENE-E siRNAs (Dharmacon J-012345-05, J-012345-07), non-targeting control siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01), or siRNA against GENE-F, a known activator of GENE-G (positive control), all using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX (Thermo 13778150). Efficient GENE-E knockdown was confirmed by RT-qPCR in a preliminary experiment. For rescue, cells were co-transfected with siRNA-resistant GENE-E cDNA carrying silent mutations at both target sites, or with empty vector. Western blotting showed that rescue protein levels were comparable to endogenous GENE-E (anti-GENE-E, Abcam ab123456, RRID:AB_2345678, 1:1000, validated in GENE-E-knockout HEK293T cells; β-actin loading control, Sigma A5441, RRID:AB_476744, 1:5000; bands within the linear range). GENE-G mRNA was normalized to TBP and PPIA (geNorm-validated) by 2^-ΔΔCt; primers are listed in Table S4. Data from n = 3 independent transfections are shown as mean ± SD and were analysed by one-way ANOVA with Tukey's test, with exact adjusted p-values reported.
