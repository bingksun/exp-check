## Text

GENE-H was knocked out in U2OS cells (ATCC HTB-96; STR-authenticated 09/2023; mycoplasma-negative; passages 6-20). U2OS cells stably expressing eSpCas9(1.1) were transduced with sgH-1 or sgH-2 targeting exons 2 and 4 (CRISPOR specificity scores 89 and 92); the control line was the same Cas9 line transduced with an AAVS1-targeting sgRNA and selected identically (puromycin 1 ug/ml, 5 days). Two single-cell clones per guide were analysed alongside the bulk pools. The locus was Sanger-sequenced in all clones (biallelic frameshifts) and protein loss was confirmed by western blot (anti-GENE-H, Bethyl A300-123A, RRID:AB_2115678, 1:2000; beta-actin loading control; signal absent) and immunofluorescence. The six top predicted off-targets per guide were amplicon-sequenced; no edits were found. A lentiviral PAM-mutated GENE-H-FLAG cDNA, expressed at 1.2-fold endogenous levels, or empty vector was used for rescue. DNA-repair reporter activity was measured in n = 4 independent experiments (separate thaws); mean +/- SD, Shapiro-Wilk-confirmed normality, one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's correction versus AAVS1 control (sgH-1 p = 0.0008; sgH-2 p = 0.0014; rescue p = 0.37).
