## Text

A premature stop codon was introduced into exon 3 of GENE-D in human iPSCs (WTC-11; normal karyotype; mycoplasma-negative; passages 24-34) by nucleofection of cytidine base editor BE4max (Addgene #112093) together with sgD-1 (5'-ACCTGGAACGTTCAGGTAAC-3'). The isogenic parental line nucleofected with BE4max plus a non-targeting sgRNA was carried through identical single-cell cloning, expansion and media changes. One homozygous edited clone (D-KO1) was selected after Sanger sequencing of the target amplicon (C>T at protospacer position 6 on both alleles; no indels), and GENE-D protein was undetectable by western blot (anti-GENE-D, Proteintech 20123-1-AP, RRID:AB_10694345, 1:1500; vinculin loading control). Cardiomyocytes were differentiated from D-KO1 and control iPSCs in four independent differentiations initiated from separate thaws. Spontaneous beating rate was measured automatically (CardioExcyte96, identical analysis settings for all wells). Data are mean +/- SD of n = 4 independent differentiations; normality was confirmed by Shapiro-Wilk and groups were compared by unpaired two-tailed t-test (p = 0.001).
