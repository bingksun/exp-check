1. Use female C57BL/6J mice (Charles River, strain code 632; 8–10 weeks old); all procedures are covered by approved protocol AUP-19-112. Randomize mice to groups using a random-number table (n = 8 mice per group; each mouse is an independent replicate).
2. Group 1: normal drinking water, no gavage. Group 2: 2.5% DSS (MP Biomedicals 160110) in drinking water for 7 days plus compound Z (20 mg/kg/day, oral gavage in 0.5% CMC). Group 3: 2.5% DSS plus mesalazine (100 mg/kg/day, oral gavage in 0.5% CMC; positive control).
3. On day 8, collect the distal colon, fix in 10% neutral-buffered formalin, embed and cut 5 µm sections.
4. Stain with H&E and assign histology scores (0–12, Dieleman scale) with the observer blinded to group codes.
5. For immunohistochemistry, incubate sections with anti-CD45 antibody (1:200, overnight, 4 °C) and quantify CD45+ cells per field in QuPath v0.4 using one fixed threshold for all slides.
6. Compare groups by Kruskal–Wallis test with Dunn's correction; plot median and interquartile range with individual mice and report exact adjusted p-values.
