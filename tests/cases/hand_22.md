## Text

GENE-A knockout clones were generated in HEK293T cells using two independent sgRNAs targeting exons 2 and 3 (sequences in Table S1). Two clones per sgRNA were isolated; frameshift alleles were confirmed by Sanger sequencing of both alleles, and loss of protein was confirmed by Western blot. Control clones were derived in parallel using a non-targeting sgRNA and underwent the same selection and single-cell cloning. All four knockout clones showed a 1.8–2.3-fold increase in apoptosis under serum starvation, which was reversed by stable re-expression of sgRNA-resistant GENE-A. The top five predicted off-target sites for each sgRNA were sequenced and found unmodified. n = 3 independent experiments per clone; two-way ANOVA.
