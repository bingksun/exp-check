Protocol 2. Hypoxia-responsive gene expression in HK-2 cells. 1. Seed HK-2 cells (ATCC CRL-2190; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by MycoAlert, passages 8-16) in 6-well plates at 3 × 10^5 per well. 2. Add 0.1% DMSO to normoxic and hypoxic wells, and 1 mM DMOG (Sigma D3695) in 0.1% DMSO to positive-control wells. Incubate hypoxic plates at 1% O2 and matched plates from the same seeding at 21% O2 (normoxic control) for 16 h. 3. Extract RNA (RNeasy Mini, Qiagen 74104, on-column DNase) and reverse-transcribe 1 µg with the High-Capacity cDNA kit (Applied Biosystems 4368814), including no-RT controls. 4. Quantify CA9 and VEGFA by SYBR Green qPCR (primer sequences and efficiencies in Table S1), in technical triplicate. 5. Normalize to RPLP0 and TBP, which are stable under hypoxia (geNorm M < 0.3), and calculate fold change versus normoxia by 2^-ΔΔCt. 6. Perform three independent experiments from separate seedings on different days (n = 3 biological replicates), averaging technical wells first. 7. Analyse each gene by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's test versus normoxia; report mean ± SD and exact adjusted p-values.
