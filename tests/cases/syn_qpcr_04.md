Figure 3. Time course of GENE-D expression after compound Z treatment. MCF-7 cells (ATCC HTB-22; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-tested, passages 10-18) were exposed to 2 µM compound Z (dissolved in DMSO, final 0.1%), and separate dishes were harvested at 0, 6, 24 and 48 h. Treatment with 10 nM 17β-estradiol for 24 h served as positive control for GENE-D induction. GENE-D mRNA was measured by SYBR Green RT-qPCR (primers in Table S2), normalized to the geometric mean of RPLP0 and GUSB (stable across time points by geNorm, M < 0.5), and expressed by 2^-ΔΔCt relative to the 0 h sample, which received neither compound nor DMSO. n = 3 independent experiments, each from a separately seeded culture. Error bars are shown. Differences versus 0 h were tested by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's multiple-comparison test; asterisks indicate significant differences.
