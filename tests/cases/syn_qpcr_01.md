A549 cells (ATCC CCL-185; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 6-14) were treated for 24 h with 10 µM compound Z, 0.1% DMSO vehicle, or 1 µM dexamethasone as a positive control for GENE-A induction. Total RNA was isolated with the RNeasy Mini Kit (Qiagen 74104) including on-column DNase digestion, and 1 µg was reverse-transcribed with the High-Capacity cDNA Reverse Transcription Kit (Applied Biosystems 4368814). qPCR used PowerUp SYBR Green Master Mix (Applied Biosystems A25742) on a QuantStudio 5; primer sequences are listed in Table S1. GENE-A expression was normalized to the geometric mean of RPLP0 and TBP, whose stability across conditions was confirmed with geNorm (M < 0.4), and calculated by the 2^-ΔΔCt method relative to vehicle. RNA was harvested from one culture dish per condition, and each cDNA was amplified in three wells; these three wells (n = 3) were analysed by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post-hoc test against vehicle.
