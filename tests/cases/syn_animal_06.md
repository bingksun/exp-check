## Text

Male C57BL/6J mice (Jackson Laboratory 000664; 8 weeks old) received CCl4 (0.5 ml/kg in olive oil, i.p., twice weekly) for 6 weeks under licence PP7654321. From week 3, mice were randomized using a random-number generator to daily oral vehicle (0.5% CMC), compound Z (30 mg/kg) or pirfenidone (300 mg/kg; positive control) in the same vehicle (n = 8 mice per group). Liver hydroxyproline was measured by colorimetric assay (Sigma-Aldrich MAK008): right-lobe tissue from all eight mice in each group was pooled, and the pooled homogenate was assayed in three wells (n = 3 per group). Picrosirius red-stained sections were assigned Ishak fibrosis scores by eye by the investigator who administered the treatments, with access to group labels. Hydroxyproline was compared by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's test against vehicle, and Ishak scores (n = 8 mice per group) by Kruskal–Wallis test with Dunn's correction. Bars show mean ± SD (hydroxyproline) or median with interquartile range (Ishak); exact adjusted p-values are given.
