Primary dermal fibroblasts were isolated from skin biopsies of two healthy donors (ethics approval REC 21/LO/0456, written informed consent; passages 3-6, mycoplasma-negative). Cells from each donor were split and treated in parallel for 48 h with 1 µM compound Z or 0.1% DMSO vehicle; 5 ng/mL TGF-β1 (R&D Systems 240-B) served as a positive control for COL1A1 induction. RNA was purified with the RNeasy Plus Mini Kit (Qiagen 74134), and 500 ng was reverse-transcribed with SuperScript IV VILO (Invitrogen 11756050). COL1A1 and ACTA2 were quantified with TaqMan assays Hs00164004_m1 and Hs00426835_g1, normalized to the geometric mean of B2M and YWHAZ (stability confirmed by NormFinder), and expressed as 2^-ΔΔCt relative to vehicle. Technical triplicates were averaged per sample, giving n = 2 biological replicates (donors) per condition. Compound Z and vehicle were compared with an unpaired two-tailed Student's t-test; data are shown as mean ± SD with exact p-values.
