## Text

Cells were immunostained for marker M and imaged by confocal microscopy. Fluorescence intensity was quantified in ImageJ using a manually set threshold. Thirty cells from one coverslip per condition were analyzed. Marker M intensity was significantly higher in treated cells (p < 0.0001, t-test).

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): 30个细胞来自同一张盖玻片，是伪重复，n应为独立实验次数；人工设阈值且未盲，有主观偏倚；没有说明各条件成像参数一致；只做了一次实验；极小的p值正是伪重复的典型表现
- 映射：30个细胞来自同一张盖玻片、只做一次实验→R02；极小p值来自伪重复→R03；人工阈值未盲、成像参数未说明一致→R05。
