## Text

Neuro-2a cells (ATCC CCL-131; STR-authenticated; mycoplasma-negative; passages 5-15) were electroporated with recombinant Cas9 and either sgF-1 or sgF-2, which target exons 2 and 7 of GENE-F (designed with GuideScan2; the four top-ranked predicted off-targets per guide were Sanger-sequenced and were wild type). Two single-cell-derived clones per guide (four clones total) were expanded. Parental Neuro-2a cells, mock-electroporated without Cas9 or sgRNA and single-cell cloned and expanded in parallel, served as the control. Biallelic frameshift alleles were confirmed by sequencing of the locus in every clone, and protein F was undetectable by western blot (anti-GENE-F, Santa Cruz sc-33333, RRID:AB_627988, 1:500; alpha-tubulin loading control). Neurite length was quantified with an automated ImageJ pipeline applied identically to all images. Re-expression of an sgRNA-resistant GENE-F cDNA, but not empty vector, restored neurite length. Data are mean +/- SD of n = 5 independent cultures per clone; Kruskal-Wallis with Dunn's correction; exact p-values are in Table S3.
