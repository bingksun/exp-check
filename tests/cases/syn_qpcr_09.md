## Text

Figure 6. GENE-H knockdown reduces GENE-J mRNA. HeLa cells (ATCC CCL-2; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 7-15) were transfected for 72 h using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX with 10 nM of one of the following: non-targeting siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-10), either of two non-overlapping GENE-H siRNAs (Ambion s12345, s12346), or siRNA against GENE-K, a known GENE-J activator (positive control). (A) Western blot of lysates harvested in parallel with the RNA. Blots were probed with anti-GENE-H (Cell Signaling 1234S, RRID:AB_10699999, 1:1000) and anti-GAPDH as loading control (Proteintech 60004-1-Ig, RRID:AB_2107436, 1:10000); bands were within the linear range. GENE-H protein was reduced by 81% and 76%. (B) GENE-J mRNA by SYBR Green RT-qPCR (primers in Table S6), normalized to PPIA and TBP (geNorm-stable) and expressed by 2^-ΔΔCt versus non-targeting siRNA. (C) Rescue in s12345-treated cells with siRNA-resistant GENE-H cDNA (silent mutations, near-endogenous protein level) versus empty vector. Bars show mean ± SD, n = 3 independent transfections. One-way ANOVA with Tukey's test; exact adjusted p-values are shown.
