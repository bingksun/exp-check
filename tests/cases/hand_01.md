## Text

HeLa cells were transfected with 50 nM siRNA targeting GENE-X using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX. After 48 h, knockdown was confirmed by qPCR (70% reduction in GENE-X mRNA, n = 2). GENE-X-depleted cells showed a 45% reduction in proliferation measured by CellTiter-Glo at 72 h, indicating that GENE-X is required for HeLa cell growth.

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): 只用了一条siRNA，不能排除脱靶；没有non-targeting对照siRNA（通篇没提对照组是什么）；只验证了mRNA，没验证蛋白；n=2且没有统计检验；没有rescue，"required for growth"结论过强
- 映射：只用一条siRNA→R06；没有non-targeting对照→R07；只验证mRNA没验证蛋白→R08；n=2→R02；没有统计检验→R03；没有rescue、结论过强→R11。R04（报告细节）容忍。
