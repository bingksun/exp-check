## Text

For CRISPRi, A549 cells stably expressing dCas9-KRAB were transduced with an sgRNA targeting the GENE-B transcription start site. GENE-B expression was reduced by 85% (qPCR). Compared with parental A549 cells, GENE-B-repressed cells showed increased sensitivity to cisplatin (IC50 2.1 µM vs 6.4 µM).

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): 对照应为dCas9-KRAB加非靶向sgRNA的细胞，而不是亲本A549，dCas9-KRAB表达和慢病毒转导本身可能改变药敏；只用一条sgRNA；IC50没有重复次数和置信区间；蛋白水平未验证
- 映射：对照应为dCas9-KRAB加非靶向sgRNA而非亲本、只用一条sgRNA→R09；IC50没有重复次数→R02；没有置信区间→R04；蛋白未验证→R08。R03、R10、R11容忍。
