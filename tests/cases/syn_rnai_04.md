## Text

U2OS cells (ATCC HTB-96; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 6-18) were transduced at MOI 0.5 with lentivirus encoding two non-overlapping shRNAs against GENE-D (Sigma MISSION TRCN0000123456 and TRCN0000123459) or the MISSION non-targeting control shRNA (SHC002); all viruses were produced in the same batch and the lines were selected with 2 ug/ml puromycin for 5 days in parallel. Target depletion was checked by semi-quantitative RT-PCR (GENE-D primers listed in Table S2, with an ACTB amplicon amplified in parallel), which gave a visibly weaker GENE-D band in both shRNA lines. Transwell migration was quantified 10 days after transduction in three independent transductions performed on separate weeks (n = 3 biological replicates; mean +/- SEM) and analysed by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post-hoc test against the non-targeting line (shD-1 p = 0.002; shD-2 p = 0.006). Re-expression of an shRNA-resistant GENE-D cDNA, with empty vector alongside, restored migration.
