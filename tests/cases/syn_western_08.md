## Text

Jurkat cells (ATCC TIB-152; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 5–14) were stimulated for 30 min with compound Z (1 µM), 0.1% DMSO vehicle, or PMA (50 ng/mL) plus ionomycin (1 µM) as positive control, each in 0.1% DMSO. Lysates (25 µg) were immunoblotted with anti-phospho-protein T Thr202 (Abcam ab67890, clone EPR2222, RRID:AB_2345678, 1:1000; signal abolished by lambda phosphatase and absent in GENE-T knockout Jurkat lysate), anti-total protein T (Abcam ab11111, RRID:AB_2111111, 1:1000) and anti-HSP90 (Cell Signaling 4877, clone C45G5, RRID:AB_2233307, 1:2000) as loading control. Signals were acquired on a ChemiDoc within the linear range and quantified in Image Lab 6.1 with identical automated lane and background settings. Phospho-protein T was normalized to total protein T and HSP90. Data are mean ± SD of n = 4 independent experiments (cultures stimulated on separate days). After Shapiro–Wilk normality testing, groups were compared by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's multiple-comparison test versus vehicle; exact adjusted p-values are reported in the figure.
