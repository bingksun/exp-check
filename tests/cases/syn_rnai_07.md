## Text

Synchronised L1 N2 (Bristol) hermaphrodites were transferred to NGM plates seeded with HT115(DE3) expressing dsRNA against gene-a from two non-overlapping fragments (Ahringer library clone I-3F07 and an independently cloned 3' fragment in L4440, both sequence-verified) or with HT115 carrying empty L4440, induced in parallel with 1 mM IPTG for 4 h and used at the same OD600. Plates were randomised and coded, and brood size was scored at 20 C by an investigator blinded to the feeding condition. Knockdown was measured by RT-qPCR on worms collected at the scoring time point (delta-delta-Ct; reference genes cdc-42 and pmp-3, stability confirmed by geNorm): 82% and 78% transcript reduction. No antibody against protein A exists, so protein loss could not be assessed. A single-copy MosSCI gene-a rescue transgene restored brood size. Two independent feeding experiments were performed (n = 2 biological replicates, 20 animals each); medians with interquartile range are shown and groups were compared by Mann-Whitney U test (p = 0.004).
