## Text

Eight-week-old C57BL/6J mice of both sexes were randomly assigned (block randomization by litter and sex) to vehicle (5% DMSO in saline, i.p.) or compound Z (10 mg/kg, i.p., daily) for 14 days, n = 10 per group. A sample size of 8 per group was calculated to detect a 30% difference with 80% power at α = 0.05 based on pilot variance; 10 were used to allow for attrition. Tumor volume was measured twice weekly by an investigator blinded to group allocation. The pre-specified primary endpoint was tumor volume at day 14, analyzed by unpaired t-test after confirming normality (Shapiro–Wilk); growth curves were analyzed by a mixed-effects model. All procedures were approved by the institutional IACUC (protocol #2026-0417).
