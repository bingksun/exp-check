## Text

A549 cells (ATCC CCL-185; STR-authenticated in-house, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 8-16) were reverse-transfected with 25 nM siGENE-A (Dharmacon J-003456-09) or 25 nM non-targeting control siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01) using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX (Thermo 13778150) for 48 h; both arms received identical reagent volumes, siRNA concentrations and incubation times. Depletion was quantified by immunoblot with anti-GENE-A (Abcam ab12345, RRID:AB_123456, 1:1000; signal absent in GENE-A knockout lysates) and anti-GAPDH (Cell Signaling 5174, 1:5000) as loading control, using non-saturated scans of the same lysates used for the assay: 82 +/- 4% reduction of GENE-A protein at the time of readout. Proliferation was measured by CellTiter-Glo 72 h after transfection in four independent transfections performed on separate days from separate cell thaws (n = 4 biological replicates). Bars show mean +/- SD. Normality was confirmed (Shapiro-Wilk) and the two groups compared by two-tailed unpaired Student's t-test; p = 0.0031.
