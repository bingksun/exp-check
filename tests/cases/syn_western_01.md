## Text

HepG2 cells (ATCC HB-8065; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 8–15) were treated for 6 h with compound Z (5 µM), 0.1% DMSO vehicle, or the known protein P activator compound K (100 µM; positive control), all in 0.1% DMSO. Lysates (20 µg per lane) were resolved on 4–12% Bis-Tris gels, transferred to PVDF and probed with anti-phospho-protein P Ser79 (Cell Signaling Technology 11818, clone D7D11, RRID:AB_2687505, 1:1000), whose signal is lost in GENE-P knockout lysate. Bands were imaged on a LI-COR Odyssey within the linear range and quantified in Image Studio 5.2 with identical automated background subtraction for all lanes. Phospho-protein P intensity is expressed relative to the vehicle lane on each membrane. Data are mean ± SD of n = 4 independent experiments (separately plated and treated cultures). Normality was confirmed by Shapiro–Wilk, and the three groups were compared by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post-hoc test versus vehicle; exact adjusted p-values are reported.
