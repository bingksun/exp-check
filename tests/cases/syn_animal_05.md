Figure 5. Compound Z lowers protein P in orthotopic tumours. (A) Western blot of protein P in tumour lysates (30 µg total protein per lane) from female BALB/c nude mice (Envigo 069; 6–8 weeks old; licence PP2468101) bearing MDA-MB-231 orthotopic tumours (ATCC HTB-26; STR-profiled, mycoplasma-negative, passages 8–14).
