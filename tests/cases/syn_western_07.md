Hypoxic induction of protein H in HCT116 cells. 1. Seed HCT116 cells (ATCC CCL-247; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 6–14) in 6-well plates. 2. Add 0.1% DMSO to wells for normoxia (negative control) and for 1% O2 hypoxia. Add 1 mM DMOG in 0.1% DMSO to normoxic positive-control wells. Incubate all wells for 8 h. 3. Lyse directly in Laemmli buffer, load 20 µg per lane and transfer to nitrocellulose. 4. Probe with anti-protein H (Santa Cruz) and with anti-β-actin (Sigma A5441, clone AC-15, RRID:AB_476744, 1:10000) as loading control. Confirm the absence of saturated pixels in Image Lab. 5. Quantify protein H/β-actin with identical automated lane and background settings for all samples. 6. Repeat on four separate days using independently seeded cultures (n = 4). Plot mean ± SD and test normality by Shapiro–Wilk. Compare the three conditions by one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc test, and mark significant differences with asterisks.
