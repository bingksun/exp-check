## Text

To confirm specificity, GENE-X knockdown cells (siRNA #1) were transfected with a plasmid encoding full-length human GENE-X cDNA (pcDNA3.1-GENE-X) or empty vector. Re-expression of GENE-X partially restored proliferation (from 45% to 70% of control).

## Notes

- Original answer (handwritten): rescue构建没有做成siRNA抗性（靶区同义突变），siRNA会同时敲低外源cDNA，所谓部分rescue可能只是过表达量压过了敲低；没有检测rescue条件下GENE-X实际蛋白水平；仍只针对一条siRNA；没有n和统计
- 映射：rescue构建不抗siRNA、rescue条件下蛋白水平未测→R11；仍只针对一条siRNA→R06；没有n和统计→R02、R03。本段没提对照siRNA（R07）和敲低验证（R08），属于节选未提及，容忍。
