## Text

Figure 3. GENE-C depletion reduces neurite outgrowth in SH-SY5Y cells. Cells were transfected with non-targeting siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-10, 30 nM) or with siGENE-C-1 (Ambion Silencer Select s12345) or siGENE-C-2 (s12347), both at 30 nM and delivered with the same RNAiMAX protocol for 72 h. Immunoblotting of the assay lysates showed 76% and 81% loss of protein P (anti-GENE-C, Abcam ab67890, RRID:AB_234567, 1:2000; alpha-tubulin loading control, Sigma T6199); an siRNA-resistant GENE-C construct restored outgrowth (Fig. 3E). Mean neurite length per cell was measured with an automated CellProfiler pipeline applied identically to all wells. Bars show mean +/- SD of three replicate wells from one representative transfection. All pairwise comparisons were made with two-tailed unpaired t-tests and the uncorrected p values are reported: NT vs siGENE-C-1, p = 0.008; NT vs siGENE-C-2, p = 0.013; siGENE-C-1 vs siGENE-C-2, p = 0.62.
