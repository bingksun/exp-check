## Text

MCF-7 cells (ECACC 86012803; STR-verified, mycoplasma-negative, passages 12-20) were transfected with two non-overlapping siRNAs against GENE-B (siB-1, Qiagen SI02654932; siB-2, Qiagen SI03089037) at 20 nM using Lipofectamine RNAiMAX. Control wells received RNAiMAX in Opti-MEM without nucleic acid (mock) and were handled in parallel for the same 72 h; untransfected wells were included as an additional reference. Depletion was 88% and 74% at the protein level (anti-GENE-B, Santa Cruz sc-98765, 1:500, signal lost in both knockdown lanes; beta-actin, Sigma A5441, 1:10000, loading control) in the lysates from the assay plates. Transwell migration was assessed in three independent transfections on separate days (n = 3 biological replicates); bars are mean +/- SEM. Groups were compared by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's multiple-comparison correction (siB-1 p = 0.004; siB-2 p = 0.011). An siRNA-resistant GENE-B cDNA carrying silent mutations, expressed at 1.3-fold endogenous levels alongside empty vector, restored migration to within 10% of control.
