Figure 6. Compound Z suppresses proliferation in A549 xenografts. (A) Representative Ki-67 immunohistochemistry and (B) quantification in subcutaneous A549 tumours (ATCC CCL-185; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 6–12) from male athymic nude mice (Foxn1nu, Envigo 069; 6–7 weeks old; protocol IACUC-2023-017).
