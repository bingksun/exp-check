## Text

Total RNA was extracted from three independent cultures per condition (biological replicates), DNase-treated, and reverse transcribed with random hexamers. qPCR was run in technical triplicate, which were averaged before analysis. Reference genes (RPLP0 and TBP) were selected from six candidates by geNorm as stable across conditions. Primer efficiencies were 95–104% and single products were confirmed by melt-curve analysis. Relative expression was calculated by the efficiency-corrected ΔΔCt method, and statistics were performed on ΔCt values (unpaired t-test, n = 3).
