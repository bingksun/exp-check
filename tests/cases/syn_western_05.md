## Text

U2OS cells (ATCC HTB-96; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative, passages 4–12) were reverse-transfected for 72 h using RNAiMAX with 20 nM of one of the following: either of two non-overlapping GENE-A siRNAs (Dharmacon J-012345-05, J-012345-07), non-targeting siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01), or siRNA against the known upstream regulator GENE-C (Dharmacon J-067890-06; positive control). For rescue, cells stably expressing siRNA-resistant GENE-A cDNA (silent mutations in both target sites; expression comparable to endogenous) or empty vector were transfected identically. GENE-A knockdown was confirmed as efficient by RT-qPCR 24 h post-transfection (ddCt; TBP and HPRT1 references, stable across conditions). At 72 h, lysates were immunoblotted for phospho-protein P (CST 12345, clone D1A2, RRID:AB_2799999, 1:1000; signal lost after phosphatase treatment) and GAPDH (Abcam ab8245, RRID:AB_2107448, 1:5000) as loading control, all within the linear range. Densitometry was performed in Image Lab using identical automated settings. Data are mean ± SEM of n = 3 independent transfections. After Shapiro–Wilk testing, groups were compared by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's test versus non-targeting; exact p-values are reported.
