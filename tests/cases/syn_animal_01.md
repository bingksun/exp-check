## Text

Female NOD/SCID mice (NOD.CB17-Prkdcscid/J, Jackson Laboratory 001303; 7–8 weeks old) were used under protocol IACUC-2021-044, approved by the institutional animal care committee. HCT116 cells (ATCC CCL-247; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 5–10) were injected subcutaneously (2 × 10^6 cells in 100 µl PBS:Matrigel 1:1) into the right flank. When tumours reached 100 mm3, mice were ranked by tumour volume; the first ten received vehicle (0.5% methylcellulose), the next ten compound Z (25 mg/kg) and the last ten regorafenib (10 mg/kg; positive control), all by daily oral gavage in the same vehicle. Tumours were measured with calipers three times weekly by the investigator who dosed the animals and knew the group assignments. Growth curves were analysed with a linear mixed-effects model (treatment × time, mouse as random effect) followed by Dunnett's comparisons against vehicle. n = 10 mice per group, each mouse an independent biological replicate; data are mean ± SEM with exact adjusted p-values reported.
