## Text

Male Sprague-Dawley rats (Envigo; 7 weeks old, 200–250 g) underwent L5 spinal nerve ligation under isoflurane anaesthesia; procedures were approved by the Regional Ethics Committee (permit 5.8.18-04567/2020). Fourteen days after surgery, rats were assigned by a computer-generated randomization list to vehicle (saline, i.p.), compound Z (3 mg/kg in saline, i.p.) or gabapentin (100 mg/kg in saline, i.p.; positive control), with three rats per group. Mechanical withdrawal thresholds were measured with von Frey filaments (up-down method) 2 h after dosing by an experimenter blinded to treatment. Each rat was tested five times at 5-min intervals, and all measurements were pooled, giving n = 15 measurements per group (5 measurements × 3 rats). Groups were compared by one-way ANOVA with Tukey's multiple-comparison test after normality was confirmed with the Shapiro–Wilk test. Data are presented as mean ± SD; exact adjusted p-values are reported.
