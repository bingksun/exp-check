## Text

CUT&Tag profiling. HCT116 cells (ATCC CCL-247; STR-authenticated, mycoplasma-negative by PCR, passages 8-15) were treated with 1 µM compound Z or 1 µM tazemetostat (positive control), each dissolved in DMSO, for 96 h. Untreated cells harvested at the start of treatment (day 0) served as the reference group. CUT&Tag was performed on 1 x 10^5 nuclei using anti-H3K27me3 (Cell Signaling Technology 9733, RRID:AB_2616029; 1:50; specificity confirmed on a SNAP-CUTANA K-MetStat spike-in panel), with rabbit IgG (Cell Signaling 2729, RRID:AB_1031062; 1:50) as the negative antibody control. Each condition comprised three independent biological replicates (separate cultures treated on different days). Reads were aligned to hg38 with Bowtie2 and normalized to E. coli carry-over DNA. Peaks were called with SEACR (stringent mode, IgG as control) using one automated pipeline applied identically to all samples. Differential H3K27me3 domains versus the reference group were identified with DESeq2 (Wald test, Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted P < 0.05). Bar graphs show mean ± SD of the three biological replicates.
