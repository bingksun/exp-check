Figure 4. Knockdown of GENE-E sensitizes HCT-116 cells to apoptosis. (A) Representative immunoblot of lysates collected 48 h after transfection with 25 nM non-targeting siRNA (Dharmacon D-001810-01) or either of two independent siRNAs against GENE-E (Dharmacon J-010203-05 and J-010203-07), all delivered with RNAiMAX at the same concentration and for the same time. All data are from four independent transfections performed on separate days (n = 4 biological replicates). Cells were STR-authenticated and mycoplasma-negative (passages 10-19). An siRNA-resistant GENE-E cDNA with empty-vector control reversed the caspase phenotype (Fig. S4).
