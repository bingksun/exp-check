## Text

GENE-A was knocked out in HAP1 cells (Horizon C631; STR-authenticated 03/2023; mycoplasma-negative; passages 8-18) using lentiCRISPRv2 carrying sgA-1 (5'-GCTAACGGTTCAGGACTTCA-3') or sgA-2 (5'-GTTCCAGAATGCGTTACCGA-3'), which target exons 2 and 5. Control cells received the identical vector with a non-targeting guide (sgNT) and were puromycin-selected (2 ug/ml, 7 days) in parallel. All assays used bulk-edited polyclonal populations. Guides were designed in CRISPOR and the five highest-ranked predicted off-target sites were amplicon-sequenced in both pools; no indels above background were detected. Editing was confirmed by Sanger sequencing with ICE (91% and 87% indels), and GENE-A protein was undetectable by western blot (anti-GENE-A, Abcam ab12345, RRID:AB_123456, 1:1000; ECL, non-saturated exposure); band intensities were quantified and normalised to the sgNT lane. Proliferation was measured in four independent lentiviral infections. Data are mean +/- SD; one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's correction versus sgNT (sgA-1 p = 0.004; sgA-2 p = 0.007).
